## Observation: perf bench is invalid under external CPU load; A/B old vs new bundle instead
- **Context**: #147 T8b full `test/corpus/bench.mjs` run (6.4 h) while other sessions held the 1-min load at 35–70 on 12 cores.
- **Finding**: port times inflated up to 2.3x (698066 19→42 ms, 1652 867→1573 s) with a byte-identical-to-main bundle; median `ratio` 0.15→0.25, 20 ids ok→slow from port rise alone. An interleaved A/B of two warm workers (old bundle vs new, `test/corpus/bench-worker.mjs`, alternate order per round, min-of-10) under the same load reproduced none of the >2x moves (new/old 0.86–1.27).
- **Impact**: check the 1-min load before and during a bench; a perf.json captured at load >12 is not a baseline. To separate port-code speed changes from load, build the baseline commit's bundle from `git archive` and A/B it interleaved rather than comparing against an old perf.json.
- **Confidence**: High
