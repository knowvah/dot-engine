## Reach ([model-reach.mjs](model-reach.mjs))

The change is parse-side only, so an input whose parsed `Graph` is identical
on base and head renders identically under every engine and format. The script
compares node ids and order with attributes and snapshots, edges with seq and
attributes, and the whole subgraph tree (name, seq, attrs, seeded keys,
snapshot, first declarations, defaults, node and edge membership). Planning:
0 of 1320 corpus inputs differ, 14 of 15 probes differ (`reopen13` is the
control). This is stricter than #183's `reopen-count.ts` (also 0 of 1320),
because it also covers AD-3's endpoint expansion on inputs that open each
subgraph once.
